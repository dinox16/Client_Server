IV. CACH CHAY CHUONG TRINH
---------------------------

A. DEMO CO BAN
--------------
1. Mo terminal thu nhat, chay server:
   cd server
   python server.py
   
   Output mong doi:
   [SERVER] Dang lang nghe tai 0.0.0.0:5555

2. Mo terminal thu hai, chay client Alice:
   cd client
   python
   >>> from client import SecureChatClient
   >>> alice = SecureChatClient("Alice")
   >>> alice.connect("127.0.0.1", 5555)
   
   Output:
   [ALICE] Da ket noi den server
   [ALICE] Danh sach users online: ['Alice']

3. Mo terminal thu ba, chay client Bob:
   cd client
   python
   >>> from client import SecureChatClient
   >>> bob = SecureChatClient("Bob")
   >>> bob.connect("127.0.0.1", 5555)
   
   Output:
   [BOB] Da ket noi den server
   [BOB] Danh sach users online: ['Alice', 'Bob']

4. Alice gui tin nhan cho Bob:
   >>> alice.send_message("Bob", "Xin chao Bob!")
   
   Terminal cua Bob hien thi:
   [Alice]: Xin chao Bob!

5. Bob tra loi:
   >>> bob.send_message("Alice", "Chao Alice! Ban khoe khong?")
   
   Terminal cua Alice hien thi:
   [Bob]: Chao Alice! Ban khoe khong?

VI. CAC TINH NANG BAO MAT
--------------------------

A. END-TO-END ENCRYPTION (E2EE)
-------------------------------
- Tin nhan duoc ma hoa TRUOC KHI gui qua mang
- Server chi thay ciphertext, KHONG the doc plaintext
- Chi nguoi nhan co private key moi giai ma duoc

Chung minh:
1. Bat goi tin tren Wireshark
2. Chi thay hex data ma hoa
3. Khong the doc noi dung

B. DIGITAL SIGNATURE
--------------------
- Moi tin nhan duoc ky boi nguoi gui
- Nguoi nhan xac thuc chu ky truoc khi hien thi
- Ngan chan gia mao tin nhan

Chung minh:
1. Thu sua doi tin nhan tren duong truyen
2. Chu ky khong hop le
3. Client tu choi hien thi

C. BLOCKCHAIN INTEGRITY
-----------------------
- Moi tin nhan luu thanh 1 block
- Block lien ket voi nhau bang hash
- Khong the sua doi lich su

Chung minh:
1. Xem blockchain: client.blockchain.print_chain()
2. Thu sua block cu: chain[1].data = "hack"
3. Kiem tra: is_chain_valid() tra ve False

D. PHONG CHONG MALWARE
----------------------
Ngay ca khi may bi chiem quyen, attacker van KHONG THE:
- Doc plaintext message (khong co private key)
- Gia mao tin nhan (khong the tao chu ky hop le)
- Sua lich su chat (blockchain phat hien thay doi)

IX. CAU HOI THUONG GAP
-----------------------

Q: Tai sao dung ca RSA va AES?
A: RSA cham nhung bao mat, AES nhanh. Ket hop hai cai de co ca toc do va bao mat.

Q: Blockchain o day khac Bitcoin the nao?
A: Don gian hon, khong co consensus giua nhieu node, chi dung de luu lich su tin nhan.

Q: Neu mat private key thi sao?
A: Khong the giai ma tin nhan cu. Phai tao key pair moi.

Q: Server co the doc tin nhan khong?
A: KHONG. Tin nhan da duoc ma hoa E2EE truoc khi gui, server chi relay ciphertext.
